Questions answered

The right support starts with a conversation.

LEA's approach can be adapted to leadership level, business needs and the scale of your organization.

What is the difference between coaching and leadership training?

Coaching supports individual reflection, development and decision-making over time. Leadership training builds shared knowledge and capabilities through facilitated learning. LEA can help explore the most relevant combination for your goals.

Can programs be tailored for our leadership team?

Yes. A discovery conversation can help identify the relevant development areas, intended outcomes, participants and format.

Do you also support individuals and families?

LEA's broader offering includes individual and family-focused support. This website highlights corporate services first. You can explore the complete portfolio on LEA's current website.
